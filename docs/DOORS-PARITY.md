# Interactive native entrances

References: [Web `src/map/doors.ts` at v24.42.0](https://github.com/lijiabao1998/defendtaipei/blob/b9b0a2736642b37dd45925a4719dfef08b8f36b4/src/map/doors.ts) and the `setBarricade` / `barricade` functions in [Web `src/core/main.ts`](https://github.com/lijiabao1998/defendtaipei/blob/b9b0a2736642b37dd45925a4719dfef08b8f36b4/src/core/main.ts). Native implementation: [`doors.rs`](../game/src/doors.rs), [`map/collide.rs`](../game/src/map/collide.rs), [`worldplay.rs`](../game/src/worldplay.rs) and [`save.rs`](../game/src/save.rs).

## Player-facing behavior

- Near an entrance, the contextual action becomes **開門 / Open door** or **關門 / Close door**. The same reachable target drives the HUD, brass ground ring and Interact action. A closer reachable cabinet or water fixture takes priority; a closed door prevents reaching supplies through the leaf.
- New games begin with closed street entrances and an open home exit. Red home gates and green shop leaves swing outward over 0.4 seconds. A door cannot close while the player or a living NPC occupies its aperture.
- A closed entrance blocks player and NPC movement, sight and chest-height shots. Door height remains 2.4 m. This is a deliberate native extension: the pinned Web leaf code blocks only the player, leaving soldiers' original pathing unchanged.
- Doors are identified by the existing building IDs. Opening, closing, streaming out and returning do not change their state. Save and Continue preserve the closed IDs. Older native saves lacking this field retain their formerly open passages; a restored character already occupying an aperture keeps their exact position and that aperture opens safely.
- Within 3 m of a reachable doorway, the inventory's contextual **封門 / Barricade** button or keyboard **B** installs boards. It requires a carried hammer and one plank; the plank is spent and the hammer remains. A player or living NPC in the threshold prevents construction without spending materials. The technician starts with the required tools.
- A barricade closes the door and prevents ordinary Interact from reopening it. Three visible wooden boards replace the leaf. The same inventory action or **B** removes the boards and returns exactly one plank, leaving an ordinary closed door. Removal needs no hammer, and the paid plank is always returned even if later scavenging has filled the bag, following the Web's `addItem` refund.
- Building makes noise within 18 m, removal within 10 m, reduced by rain using the existing combat rule. Nearby active patrols actually enter their investigation state. This is gameplay noise, not just a sound effect or warning sentence.
- Barricade IDs are a separate defaulted save field. Validation rejects unknown/duplicate IDs and barricades whose door is not closed. Continue restores the same shared movement/combat state even before any meshes stream in. If an old or manually positioned snapshot puts the player inside a barricade aperture, restoration opens that aperture, refunds its paid plank once, and preserves the player's exact position.

## Rendering and collision bounds

Static frames, sills and address plates remain part of the building chunks. Live leaves use four shared meshes and two shared materials; at most 24 doors within 48 m are present. Barricades add one shared 360-vertex mesh and at most one extra entity for each of those same 24 slots. They are created only when their building's Near or Mid owner chunk exists, fade with its walls, and shorten with the interior cutaway. Supporting Mid owners prevents a large footprint's distant centroid from hiding a nearby entrance leaf. LOD changes never change collision state. The closed-door collision index uses 8 m cells shared by cheap `Obstacles` clones, including the boxed combat world; queries do not scan every city door. Closed and barricaded sets are updated under one lock, so a clone never observes boards on an open passage.

Movement uses circle substeps so a long frame cannot jump over the closed aperture. The ring and leaf swing have no separate gameplay collision. The state becomes open or closed immediately when the interaction succeeds.

## Boundaries and checks

This subset does not add building locks, damage to doors or enemy door-opening AI. The pinned Web's lockpicking skill is used by vehicles; it does not define a building-lock system. New door art is procedural mesh geometry. Building IDs and save version stay unchanged.

Unit checks cover shared movement/LOS/shot collision, large-step crossing, open-leaf passage width, occupied-threshold refusal, target selection on both sides, water access behind a closed arcade door, bounded visual geometry, saved state independent of render slots, legacy save fields, invalid IDs and safe Continue at a threshold. Barricade tests additionally check tool/material conservation, reach and occupancy before spending, ordinary-open rejection, removal refunds, actual patrol investigation from a gameplay message, and real disk-save/Continue restoration. Runtime `DTDOOR barricaded=<bool> id=<building> plank=<count>` records only successful actions for QA. End-to-end screenshots and Android smoke results should be read from the CI run for the installed revision.
